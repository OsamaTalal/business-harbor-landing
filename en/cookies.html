Cookie policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how the Business Harbor page uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

1What are cookies?

Small text files stored in your browser when you visit the page. They help it work, remember your choices and measure performance.

2Cookies we use

  • Essential: to run the page and remember your choice. Always on.
  • Analytics (optional): to understand and improve how the page is used, e.g. Google Analytics via Google Tag Manager.
  • Advertising (optional): to measure our campaigns, e.g. Google Ads and Meta tools.

3Your consent

Analytics and advertising cookies are only enabled after you accept them in the cookie banner. If you choose “Reject non-essential”, only essential cookies are used.

4Changing your choice

Use the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the main page at any time, or delete cookies in your browser settings.

5Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.
